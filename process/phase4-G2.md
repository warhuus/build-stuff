## QUESTIONS.md entries (for G5)
- **Assumed (spec §9.0 `fetchAllPages`, lead note L1):** `capped` is true whenever the fetched rows reach `ROW_CAP`, even if the last page ended exactly at the cap and nothing was cut. Both adapters and the chunk merge follow the spec's pseudocode (`if rows.length >= ROW_CAP: capped = true`). A result of exactly ROW_CAP rows is therefore reported `partial` + `row-cap`.
- **Assumed (instructions §7, §10; TYP-07):** every abort, from any layer, is a `DOMException` named `AbortError` with the message `"aborted"`, so `MetricResult.error` reads `"aborted"`.
- **Assumed (spec §9 4.1 step 3, D15):** OSDK verdict dates are converted to the UTC calendar date (`window.dateOnlyOrNull`). An unparsable or empty date becomes `null` (the verdict then counts as missing its date) and does not fail the load.
