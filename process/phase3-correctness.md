## Semantic edge cases checked (no finding)

- **Tie rule.** `compareEvents` orders opened < human < closed. `closureGroupOf` keeps human events with ts ≤ closedAt, so A44 is viewOnly. `firstViewToClosureHours` keeps 0 h. A13 (view at the opened stamp) gives 0 h in 4.3. A41 (op = cl) gives 0 h in 4.2.
- **Closure precedence with events after closure.** Events after `closedAt` are ignored: A53 and A54 are noHuman in 4.6, and noHuman comes from the `closedTotal − touched` remainder, clamped at 0 (W7).
- **Reopened alerts.** A29, A30 and A31 are open now, so `isClosed` is false, `closureGroup` is null, and they are excluded from 4.2, 4.4 and 4.6. `raisedAt` is the minimum `opened` (spec §4), so A29 gives 507 h in 4.3 and A30 has age 60.25 d in 4.5. The attrs event is the latest `opened`, falling back to `closed`.
- **raisedAt null.** 4.2 and 4.3 count it as `noRaise` in both series (A40 at 7 and 14 d, A56 under now). 4.5 counts it in `unknownAge`, and the threshold pct uses known ages only (O6).
- **Unscored by subtraction.** Unscored count and value = total − Σ ranges − delayed, clamped (`riskSideTotals`). Not worked = all − worked, clamped (`bucketRows`, `bucketAmountsOutside`); b51_70 not worked is 0, not negative.
- **2.1 alert-view term b.** b is open − open-with-lifecycle, grouped on AOF, so it is disjoint from term a. Sums were checked per group, for example 27 + 16 + 14 = 57.
- **2.3 and 2.4 outside paths.** Item view: `so21 ∩ acted − so22` and `so21 ∩ wb − so23`. Alert view: `acted ∖ viewed` and `wb ∖ stage23`, always computed (F4). The alert-view caveat is COR-01.
- **overlapRatio.** Σ over ALL ranking groups before truncation, divided by the first applicable stage total. The item-view alert dim ranks on 2.1 with every candidate; the userFunnel dim ranks on its first stage (1.1, 1.2, 1.3 or 1.4). Additive dims always get null.
- **Top-N chosen once.** Funnels choose on the first applicable stage; durations, 4.5 and 4.6 choose on the whole population, including excluded alerts. The item-view loader queries 2.2–2.4 only for the top groups of 2.1, the same list derive shows.
- **`other` for additive dims.** Item dims, 3.1, the alert view, 4.2–4.4, 4.5 and 4.6 all put null-valued rows in `other`. `other` is null for non-additive dims.
- **4.5 item value counted once per item.** `oldestAgeByItem` and the `pastItems` set handle it; under an alert dim the value overlaps (caveat `overlap`).
- **Threshold pct denominator.** Open alerts with a known age; null at 0.
- **O7 quantiles.** Match the formula, including empty bins skipped and a null result in the open-ended bin.
- **4.1.** Gate and date are checked client-side per mode and never swapped; the denominator is made + not-made only (P3); `missingVerdict` = distinct ids without a verdict row.
